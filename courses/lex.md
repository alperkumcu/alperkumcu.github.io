## Course Description and Content

This course introduces lexicology and the study of words in the context of translation and interpreting. Students examine words as linguistic signs and explore their origins, internal structure, meaning, use in context, mental representation, and documentation in corpora and dictionaries. Topics include etymology, morphology, lexical semantics and pragmatics, psycholinguistics, corpus-based approaches to vocabulary, and lexicography. By the end of the course, students will be able to analyse words in relation to their contexts and make informed lexical choices in translation and interpreting.

## Objectives & Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Explain key concepts in lexicology and distinguish among word forms, lexemes, and lexical units.
- Describe the historical development of the English lexicon and identify borrowing and other forms of lexical innovation.
- Analyse the morphological structure of words, including inflection, derivation, and word-formation processes.
- Discuss lexical meaning, semantic relations, and the effects of context and register on word use.
- Explain basic concepts concerning the mental lexicon and lexical processing.
- Use corpora, dictionaries, and digital lexical resources to investigate words and support lexical choices in translation and interpreting.
