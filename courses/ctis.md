**Belo Horizonte, Brazil**

**Module Name:** Cognitive Psychology and Psycholinguistics for Translation and Interpreting Researchers

**Lecturer:** Alper Kumcu, PhD

| **Class** | **Title** | **Outline** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monday**<br>**Foundations** | **Inside the Translating Mind** | **What happens between encountering a message and expressing it in another language?** This class introduces mental representations and the processes supporting comprehension, memory and language production. We connect these foundations to translation and interpreting, and consider how researchers can use observable behaviour to investigate processes that cannot be observed directly. |
| **Tuesday Research landscape** | **Two Languages, One Mind** | **How do bilinguals understand and produce language while both languages remain available?** This class surveys research on bilingual lexical access, cross-language activation, prediction and language control. We examine what these approaches offer translation and interpreting researchers, and where findings from general bilingual populations may need testing in professional language mediation. |
| **Wednesday**<br>**Attention and memory** | **How Much Can the Mind Handle?** | **Why do some tasks overwhelm us while others become easier with practice?** This class explores attention, working memory and executive control in translation and interpreting. We examine competing explanations of processing difficulty and discuss how task demands, expertise and individual differences can inform hypotheses about cognitive load and performance outcomes. |
| **Thursday**<br>**My research** | **When Effort Meets Emotion** | **Does feeling stressed mean that an interpreter is working harder?** Drawing on my research on cognitive load and emotion in interpreting, this class examines relationships between task demands, subjective experience, physiological responses and performance. We discuss what agreement and disagreement between these measures can tell us about the interpreting process. |
| **Friday**<br>**Joint session with García** | **From Mind to Brain: What Counts as Evidence?** | **What can psychological and neuroscientific evidence tell us about the same translation or interpreting problem?**[^1] |
